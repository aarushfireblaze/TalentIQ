Recruiter: How do you approach a bug you have not seen before?
Sam: I try to reproduce it with the smallest input, inspect the logs, and then make one change at a time. For the sensor logger, malformed packets were shifting the readings. I wrote a parser test first, then fixed the boundary check.
Recruiter: How did your team work together?
Sam: We agreed on the packet format with the electrical team before splitting the code. I asked them to review what counts as a missing reading because I didn't know the hardware behavior.
